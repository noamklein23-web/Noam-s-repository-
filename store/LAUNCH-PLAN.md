# Launch Plan: Hebrew "Mom Necklace" Store (Israel, Shopify, under $200)

**Product:** Personalized necklace engraved with children's (or family) names in Hebrew.
**Working brand name:** **שלנו | Shelanu** ("ours"). Check that the name and domain are free before you commit (see Step 2).
**First sales target:** Family Day (Yom HaMishpacha, early Feb 2027) and Valentine's Day (Feb 14).
**Budget:** under $200, so **no paid ads**. Sales come from organic TikTok / Instagram Reels and friends and family.

> Honest expectation: most first products don't take off. The goal of this plan is to get your first
> 5–10 sales cheaply and learn fast. If after ~6 weeks of daily posting nothing converts, switch the
> product, not the whole store.

## Step-by-step (what YOU do vs. what's already done)

### Week 1 (Nov 22–29): Set up
- [ ] **You:** Open an **osek patur** (exempt dealer) at the Tax Authority (Mas Hachnasa + Ma'am). Needed for a payment gateway and receipts. Ask an accountant if unsure.
- [ ] **You:** Check the brand name: search Google, Instagram, TikTok and the Israeli trademark database for "שלנו / Shelanu". If taken, pick another (ideas: יקירי Yakiri, שמות קרובים, Ahuvim Jewelry).
- [ ] **You:** On AliExpress, search "custom name necklace" / "personalized multiple names necklace". Shortlist **3 suppliers** using `suppliers.md` from the skill: rating 95%+, 500+ orders, photo reviews, ships to Israel.
- [ ] **You:** Message each supplier using `supplier-messages.md` (already written). **Ask them to engrave a Hebrew test name.** Many get Hebrew wrong (reversed letters, broken font).
- [ ] **You:** Order **one sample from each** with a Hebrew name, e.g. "נועה · איתי".
- [ ] **You:** Start the Shopify trial. Set store language to **Hebrew**, currency to **ILS**. Use the free **Dawn** theme (supports right-to-left).
- [ ] **Done:** Product page copy → `product-page.he.md`
- [ ] **Done:** Shipping, returns, about and FAQ pages → `policies.he.md`

### Week 2 (Nov 30–Dec 6): Build the store
- [ ] **You:** Paste the product page from `product-page.he.md`. Add a **text personalization field** (free Shopify app such as a "product options / customizer" app) for: names, metal color, chain length.
- [ ] **You:** Paste the policy pages from `policies.he.md`. Have an accountant or lawyer glance at the returns policy.
- [ ] **You:** Install a payment gateway: **Allpay** or **Pelecard** from the Shopify App Store (both support Israeli cards; Allpay also supports Bit). Make one real test purchase and refund it.
- [ ] **You:** Start an Instagram and TikTok account with the brand name. Post 3 "teaser" videos from `content-scripts.md` (scripts 1–3) using phone footage and supplier listing videos *only if the supplier allows it*.

### Week 3–4 (Dec 7–20): Samples arrive, choose a supplier
- [ ] **You:** When samples arrive, score each one with the 100-point template in `.claude/skills/dropshipping/suppliers.md`. Check: Hebrew correct? Letters not mirrored? Color matches photos? Clasp OK?
- [ ] **You:** Pick the best as **primary**, second best as **backup**.
- [ ] **You:** Photograph the winner **yourself** (your own photos only, never stolen ones). Natural light, on a neck, in a gift box, close-up of the Hebrew letters.
- [ ] **You:** Replace placeholder images and **launch**. Write down the real delivery time it took, and put that on the product page.

### Week 4–10 (Dec 21–Jan 31): Get sales with no ad budget
- [ ] **Post one short video every day** (scripts in `content-scripts.md`). Consistency matters more than quality.
- [ ] **First 5 sales from people you know**: send the store to friends and family, offer a launch code (e.g. `SHELANU10`) and ask buyers for a photo or video when it arrives. Those become your reviews and content.
- [ ] Post in relevant Facebook groups (moms groups, gift-ideas groups) **only where the rules allow promotion**.
- [ ] Offer 2–3 micro-creators (mom accounts, 2k–20k followers) a free necklace with their kids' names in exchange for an honest video. Costs ~₪45 each, often the best spend at this budget.
- [ ] If one video clearly outperforms the rest (views + profile visits), boost **that video only** with ~$30–50.
- [ ] **Last safe order date for Family Day:** about **Jan 10–15**, depending on the real delivery time from your sample. Put a countdown on the site and in your posts.

### Weekly check (every Sunday)
| Metric | Healthy | Action if not |
|---|---|---|
| Views per video | Rising over time | Change the hook (first 2 seconds) |
| Store visits → orders | 1–3% | Improve photos, add reviews, check price |
| Supplier processing time | Under 5 days | Message supplier, switch to backup |
| Orders stuck with no tracking for 7+ days | 0 | Contact supplier, update the customer proactively |
| Complaints about engraving errors | 0 | Remake immediately, tighten supplier instructions |

## Rules that protect you
1. **Always show the real delivery time** ("10–21 business days", or whatever your sample took) on the product page and in the cart.
2. **Send the order details to the supplier exactly as the customer typed them**, and copy-paste Hebrew. Also send a screenshot of the names so the supplier doesn't flip the letters.
3. **Refund or remake fast** when something's wrong. One bad experience shared in a moms group costs more than a ₪45 remake.
4. **Don't promise "gold" or "silver"** unless it really is. Say "stainless steel, gold-tone plating" (or whatever the supplier confirms).
5. Keep a simple spreadsheet: order #, customer name, names to engrave, supplier order #, tracking, delivery date.
